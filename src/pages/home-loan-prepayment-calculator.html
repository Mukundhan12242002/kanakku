This assumes the EMI stays fixed and each prepayment goes straight to principal. Banks differ on how they apply it, and some charge a fee on fixed-rate or non-individual loans. Floating-rate home loans to individuals generally carry no prepayment penalty, but confirm in your sanction letter.

Interest is compounded monthly on the reducing balance. A floating rate that changes will change the result.

How this is calculated

  • EMI comes from the standard reducing-balance formula for the loan, rate and tenure you enter.
  • Each month, interest is the balance times the monthly rate. The rest of the payment reduces the balance.
  • Extra money is added to the same payment, so the balance falls faster and the loan ends earlier.

Questions people ask

Is it better to prepay early in the loan?

Yes. Early on, most of each EMI is interest, so each rupee prepaid removes interest on every later month. The same amount prepaid in the last years saves far less.

Does prepaying reduce the EMI or the tenure?

This calculator keeps the EMI and shortens the tenure, which saves the most interest. Many banks let you choose to reduce the EMI instead; ask yours.

Should I prepay the loan or invest the money?

Prepaying earns a certain return equal to your loan rate, with no market risk. Investing may earn more or less. The SIP step-up calculator shows what a monthly amount could grow to at a return you choose.
