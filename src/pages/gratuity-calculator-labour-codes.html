Kanakku / Gratuity

Gratuity under the new labour codes

பணிக்கொடை

See what you get on your pay as it is today, and what changes if your wages must be at least half of your total pay.

All pay components for the month, used only for the 50% wage test. Leave employer PF and gratuity out.

Most employers with 10 or more staff are covered. Ask HR if unsure.

What you get

Two points that are still debated

Fixed-term employees: the Ministry of Labour's FAQ says a fixed-term employee is eligible after one year of service. A legal analysis points out that the Code on Social Security says "pro rata" without naming a one-year threshold, and that it overlaps with the Industrial Relations Code. This page applies one year, as the Ministry states.

Wage definition: the 50% rule means "wages" cannot be less than half of total remuneration. Your employer may restructure your pay, or may already be compliant, so the "new codes" column is what you would get if your wage base rose to half of total pay. It is not a promise.

How this is calculated

  • Gratuity = monthly wages × 15 ÷ 26 × completed years of service.
  • A part-year of more than six months counts as a full year. Six months or less does not count.
  • Permanent employees need five years of continuous service, unless leaving because of death or disablement. This page uses full years, so ask HR or a lawyer if you are close to the line.
  • Fixed-term employees are paid pro rata on completing at least one year of the contract.
  • The tax-exempt and payable ceiling is ₹20,00,000 and is applied here.
  • The 30-day divisor is the tax-rule basis for employers not covered by the law, half a month's salary per year.
