What it adds up to

Where sources disagree, and what this page uses

Registration fee on a sale deed: a state rate chart citing G.O.(Ms.) No.34, effective 1 April 2023, shows 2%, and TNREGINET's duty table is reported at 7% stamp duty (5% duty plus 2% transfer duty) and 2% registration. Many 2026 pages still say 4%. This page uses 2%.

Women's concession: sources report a cut of one percentage point for properties up to ₹10 lakh, but they describe it differently (3% from 4%, or 1% from 2%). This page subtracts one point. Confirm at the sub-registrar's office before you rely on it.

Always compare with the figure on your sub-registrar's e-assessment. If it differs, type that rate into the box below.

Rates used (you can change them)

How this is calculated

  • Duty base is the higher of the sale price and the guideline value.
  • Stamp duty and registration fee are percentages of that base.
  • GST is charged on the sale price for under-construction purchases only. Resale and ready-to-move-in property has none.
  • Cash needed on the day is your down payment plus duties, GST and other costs. The loan covers the rest of the price.
  • EMI uses the standard reducing-balance formula, compounded monthly.

Builder-sold new apartments in some cities are registered on a composite value and can work out differently. Treat this as a planning estimate and confirm with the sub-registrar's assessment.
