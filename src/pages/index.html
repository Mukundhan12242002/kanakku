Why the numbers here come with sources

While building these, the same rate showed up as 1%, 2% and 4% on different sites, and the free electricity units as 100 and 200. Some sites are out of date, and some copy each other. Every tool here uses the figure best supported by a government order or the regulator's own tariff order, shows it in an editable box, and tells you what the other figures are.
