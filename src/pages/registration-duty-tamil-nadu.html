Kanakku / Deed duty

Stamp duty and registration fee by deed type

பத்திரப் பதிவு கட்டணம்

Pick the kind of document and enter its value. You get the stamp duty and the registration fee, with the cap that applies, so a family settlement does not get priced like a sale.

The caps apply to each share.

What it costs

What this page cannot tell you

The rates come from one consolidated state rate chart. A few rows in that chart were unreadable when it was copied, so those deeds are left out rather than guessed. Rates change by government order, so check the figure against the sub-registrar's assessment before you pay.

Whether your deed counts as a "family" deed is decided by the registration department, not by this page. In the chart, a legal heir of a deceased family member counts as family.

How this is calculated

  • Each duty is a percentage of the value you enter, a fixed rupee amount, or a percentage with a ceiling.
  • Where a ceiling applies per share, it is multiplied by the number of shares.
  • Stamp duty and registration fee are shown separately and then added.

Questions people ask

Why is a gift to a relative cheaper than a gift to a friend?

The state rate chart charges family settlements, releases and partitions 1% stamp duty (capped at ₹40,000) and 1% registration fee (capped at ₹10,000), while the same transfer to a non-family member is charged like a sale, at 7% and 2%.

What is the registration fee on a sale deed in Tamil Nadu?

The state rate chart shows 2% of market value, in force from 1 April 2023 under G.O.(Ms.) No.34, with stamp duty at 7%. Many older pages still quote 4%.

Is the value the sale price or the guideline value?

For a sale, duty is charged on the market value, which in practice means the higher of the price and the guideline value. Use the property cost calculator if you want that comparison done for you.
